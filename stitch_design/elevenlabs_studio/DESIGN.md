# ElevenLabs Studio — Updated Design System

A Bauhaus-inspired editorial design system featuring warm paper surfaces, whisper-weight typography, crisp black ink, and vibrant accent colors.

## Color Roles & Tokens

- **Ember Orange (`#ff4704`)**: Secondary accent role (`secondary`, `ember-orange`). Used for active highlights, dynamic product badges, audio visualizers, and vibrant interaction cues.
- **Violet Spark (`#0447ff`)**: Tertiary accent role (`tertiary`, `violet-spark`). Used for technical spark moments, AI indicator badges, and product art accents.
- **Eggshell Canvas (`#fdfcfc`)**: Clean, warm paper background.
- **Warm Taupe (`#f5f3f1`)**: Subtle container cards and surface bands.
- **Stone (`#ebe8e4`)**: Hairline borders and quiet dividers.
- **Ink (`#000000`)**: High-contrast headlines, filled pill buttons, and dominant UI text.
- **Smoke (`#777169`) & Graphite (`#44403b`)**: Calm, readable body and secondary editorial copy.
